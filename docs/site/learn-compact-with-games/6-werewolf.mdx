### N-player game with a host-facilitated flow

A fully peer-to-peer mental-poker-style protocol gets expensive once you
have more than a couple of players. We use a host to drive the round -
collecting commitments, ordering reveals, advancing phases - without
giving the host the ability to lie about results. This is the section
that ties the whole series together: every primitive from the earlier
games shows up here in some form.
